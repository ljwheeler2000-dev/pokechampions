# Team Build: Tyranitar/Sinistcha/Milotic Core

Built via the 7-step methodology (claude/teambuilding-methodology.md), Reg M-B doubles.

## Roster (6/6 locked)

1. **Tyranitar** (Rock/Dark) — core anchor; will hold a Mega Stone (double mega threat, see handoff #2)
2. **Sinistcha** (Grass) — core
3. **Milotic** (Water) — core
4. **Incineroar** (Fire/Dark) — fills core; patches Bug/Grass resist, brings Psychic immunity
5. **Mega Gengar** (Ghost/Poison) — fills core; Fighting/Normal immunity directly answers Tyranitar's 4x Fighting weakness; will hold a Mega Stone
6. **Sylveon** (Fairy) — closes the Dragon blind spot (immune) and adds a Fighting resist; picked over Staraptor-Mega specifically to avoid running 3 mega-capable Pokemon on one team

## Known weaknesses and overlaps (final 6)

Types hitting more than one team member:

- **Ground — 3 members (Tyranitar, Incineroar, Mega Gengar). Unresolved — the team's single biggest structural gap.** Sylveon does not address this (neutral to Ground). No Ground answer (Levitate/Flying immunity, or a dedicated resist) currently on the team.
- **Fighting — 2 members (Tyranitar 4x, Incineroar 2x).** Partially mitigated: Gengar is immune, Sylveon resists, but neither stops a Fighting move aimed at Tyranitar or Incineroar directly — this only helps via matchup/switch decisions, not raw bulk.
- **Water — 2 members (Tyranitar, Incineroar)**
- **Grass — 2 members (Tyranitar, Milotic)**
- **Bug — 2 members (Tyranitar, Sinistcha)**
- **Poison — 2 members (Sinistcha, Sylveon)** — new overlap introduced by Sylveon
- **Steel — 2 members (Tyranitar, Sylveon)** — new overlap introduced by Sylveon

Single-member weaknesses: Fire (Sinistcha), Electric (Milotic), Ice (Sinistcha), Flying (Sinistcha), Rock (Incineroar), Ghost (Mega Gengar), Dark (Mega Gengar), Fairy (Tyranitar).

No weakness: Normal. **Dragon is now covered** — Sylveon is flatly immune, closing what was previously a total gap against threats like Garchomp (S-tier per meta report).

Useful immunities already on the team: Mega Gengar (Normal, Fighting), Tyranitar + Incineroar (Psychic), Sylveon (Dragon).

## Step 6/7 handoff

1. **Ground is the priority open problem.** Consider an item-level fix (Air Balloon on one of the three exposed members) or accept the risk given only 3 of 6 are hit and none of the meta report's S-tier Ground users are called out as dominant this window.
2. **Mega slot: both Tyranitar and Gengar will hold their respective Mega Stones** (double mega threat — only one actually evolves per battle, chosen based on matchup; typing is unaffected either way since neither changes type on Mega Evolution). This keeps the team at 2 mega-capable Pokemon, not 3, consistent with the earlier decision to pass on Staraptor-Mega.
3. No dedicated speed control (Tailwind/Trick Room) or redirection (Follow Me/Rage Powder) locked in yet — worth revisiting per the doubles methodology's step 5.
4. Playtest once movesets/items/SP spreads (step 6) are set.
