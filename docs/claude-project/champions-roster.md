# Pokémon Champions Roster

Sources: Bulbapedia (M-B snapshot) + the official Reg M-C addition list.
**Current regulation: M-C, live 2026-09-09 through 2026-12-01.** Rosters rotate — recheck before
relying on this after 2026-12-01.

## How availability works
Pokémon Champions uses a rotating roster. Pokémon in the current roster are obtainable through
standard gameplay. Pokémon **not** in the current roster cannot be obtained at all without events
or transferring from Pokémon HOME. M-C carries forward the M-B roster and adds to it.

### Cross-cutting notes for teambuilding

- **Two new Intimidate traps.** Mabosstiff's Guard Dog gains +1 Atk from Intimidate, and Kingambit's
  Defiant (98.4% usage) gains +2. Double-Intimidate teams need to check the opposing lead first.
- **Two new sound checks.** Mr. Mime's Soundproof (immunity) and Toxtricity's Punk Rock (halved)
  both blunt Sylveon's Pixilate Hyper Voice, which was S tier off the back of it.
- **Court Change on Cinderace** is a live answer to Tailwind and screens.
- **Psychic Surge on Indeedee** switches off opposing priority against grounded targets — a direct
  counter to Kingambit's Sucker Punch, Grassy Glide, Ice Shard and Fake Out. Airborne Pokémon
  (Flying-types, Levitate) are not protected by it.
- **No new Quick Guard, Wide Guard or Armor Tail user** arrived in M-C.
